Questions

Do I need to send photos?

Photos help, but a description works too ("tall, curly hair, always in a hoodie"). We design cartoon characters inspired by the people in your story — not exact copies.

When do I pay?

Only after you've seen and approved your script. Then we send you a secure payment link, and animation starts once it's paid.

Can I ask for changes?

Yes — change anything in the script before we animate. After delivery, one round of small fixes (a caption, a line, a shot) is included.

How is it made?

Every story is written and directed by a real person. We animate it with AI video tools, then edit, time and score it by hand. That's how we keep it fast and affordable.

Will you post my story online?

Never without your permission. Your video is yours. If you're happy for us to share it as a sample, we'll ask first.

Can my business use the ad anywhere?

The brand package includes use on your own social media, website and in-store screens. Need paid ads or TV? Just ask.

I need it by a certain date

Put your deadline in the form. Rush orders are often possible — we'll tell you right away if we can make it.
